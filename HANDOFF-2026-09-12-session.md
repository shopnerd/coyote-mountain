16. **Photo ground seams, for real this time (12 Sept, `db04f7d`).** Will still saw a pale lattice between photo tiles (his screenshot: render, aerial on, white cut, dpr 2). Cause found by painting the under-fill red (it was not the fill): each tile's darkening is a separate polygon painted after its photo patch, and the NEXT tile's patch overlapped its neighbour by 5% and painted raw, unshaded photo back over the darkened rim. Wider overlap = wider light line. A canvas `filter: brightness()` per drawImage fixes it but is catastrophically slow (the page stalled >30 s). What works: no patch overlap at all (`ov = 0`), the fill under each patch darkened by the tile's own shade (so a hairline of it is invisible), shade polygon outset .3 px only. e2e161 (this session's scratchpad) scores light seam pixels at dpr 2: 13.4 → 5.4 per 1000; render time unchanged (182 ms per 1536×864). `window.__dbg` now also hands out `aerialCache` and `AER`.
