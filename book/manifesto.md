# Coyote Mountain

Free for learning and the commons.

## What this is for

Tools for people who look after land. Grade a pad, trace water, plant an orchard, stake a swale, count what lives there, and check whether the work did any good.

It runs in a browser. It works on a laptop in a studio and on a phone in a field. No fee, no account, nothing to install.

## What we believe

**Observe before you design.** Walk it, record it, fetch its history. The land has already solved most of its problems. Find out how before adding yours.

**The smallest intervention that does the most lasting good.** Every tool here asks that question. Leaving it as it is always counts as an answer.

**Build it right.** A good design badly built is a bad design. Stake it, check the level, hand the operator the right files, keep out of the ground that needs protecting.

**Measure what actually happened.** A swale is a hypothesis until the soil holds more water. Fly it before and after. Count what comes back. Keep the notes.

**Say what is estimated and what is measured.** Carbon ranges, forage numbers, fire risk and budget rates are starting points, and every tool labels them that way. Measurements beat models. A measurement always says how it was taken.

**The people on the land hold the data.** Your notes, counts and plans stay in your project file and your browser. The tool only reaches out when you ask it to: fetching terrain, photos, old maps or iNaturalist records, or painting a picture with your own key. Sensitive species stay private.

**Teach.** Every tool should be learnable by a student in an afternoon and still be useful to a professional. The chapters, the field cards and the syllabus are part of the work, not an afterthought.

## Free for learning and the commons

Use it, copy it, change it and share it for teaching, study, research and personal projects, and in any school, charity, environmental group, research organisation or public body. That is the point of it.

Selling it, selling a service built on it, or using it in a business needs a separate licence. That keeps it from being sold back to the people it was made for.
