# Field card 10 · Walk the paddocks and move the herd

Decide when to move animals by how the plants are doing, not by the calendar.

**People:** 1  
**Tool:** coyotemountainfarm.com/topo.html · Shape · grazing  

## Walk the paddock the herd is in

1. Look at the plants they like best. Are they grazed down to about half their height?
2. Look at the ground: bare patches growing, tracks around the water, dung spread or piled?
3. If the best plants are at half, it is time to move, whatever the plan said.

## Walk the next paddock

1. **Forage now:** sparse, fair, good or lush. Judge it by eye, or clip a square metre, dry it and weigh it.
2. Has it rested long enough? The best plants should have regrown their leaves.

## Move

1. Open the gate, move the herd, check water in the new paddock.
2. In the tool: pick the new paddock, update **forage now**, press **move the herd in today**. The last paddock starts resting.
3. Walk the old paddock once more and add a field note if anything needs attention.

## Good to know

- Grazing days in the tool are a starting estimate from area, forage and daily intake. The plants overrule it.
- Take half, leave half is the usual starting rule.
- **Leave it ungrazed** keeps a paddock out of the rotation: young trees, a recovering drainage, nesting ground.
- Rest longer in the dry season, when plants regrow slowly.

## Done when

The herd is in, water checked, and the move is recorded.
