# Field card 15 · Check what was built

Compare the finished earthwork with the design, before the machine leaves.

**People:** 2  
**Tool:** coyotemountainfarm.com/topo.html · Make · field build and Study · ground change  

## Quick check, before the machine leaves

1. Walk the stakes. Is the ground at the marked cut or fill?
2. On pads and swales, lay the board and phone level (card 01) across and along.
3. Check the keep-out zones are untouched.
4. Note anything off, with a photo, and fix it while the machine is there.

## Full check, with a survey

1. Fly the finished work (card 12) and make a terrain model.
2. Make · field build · **check an as-built survey against the design**. Ground change opens.
3. **After survey:** load the new terrain model.
4. Read it: ember is ground left below the design, blue is ground above it, with volumes.

## Good to know

- A few centimetres off is normal. Set **ignore changes under** to what you would accept.
- Look hardest at spillways, swale levels and pad edges. Those decide whether the water goes where it should.

## Done when

The work matches the design within what you accept, or the fixes are noted and scheduled.

## After

The first real rain is the final check (card 07).
