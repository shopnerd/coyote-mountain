# Field card 14 · Stake the grading for a machine

Hand a graded design to the crew: stakes on the ground marked cut or fill, surfaces for machine control, and the ground to stay out of.

**People:** 2, and the machine operator  
**Tool:** coyotemountainfarm.com/topo.html · Make · field build  

## Before the crew comes

1. Finish the grading design in Shape. Check the budget (Shape · earthwork budget).
2. Mark protected ground: Study · landscape fit (draw a protected area), recovery areas, paddocks left ungrazed.
3. Make · field build: pick **a stake every** (10 m is usual).
4. Export **stakes · csv** and **stakes · gpx**.
5. For machine control, export **design surface · landxml** and **existing surface · landxml**, and **keep-out zones · kml**.

## Set out the stakes

1. Find each stake with a GPS (the gpx), or have a surveyor set them from the csv (it has UTM coordinates).
2. Write on each stake what the csv says: **C** and the depth to cut, or **F** and the height to fill.
3. Set out the edge marks: where the work meets untouched ground.
4. Fence or tape the keep-out zones before any machine arrives.

## Before digging

1. Walk the stakes with the operator. Agree where spoil and topsoil go.
2. Strip topsoil first and pile it where it will not be buried.
3. For machine control: load the LandXML surfaces with the dealer and check them against a few stakes before digging to them.

## Good to know

- Heights come from the ground model. On a drone survey they are good; on public terrain data they are rough, so a surveyor should check the stakes.
- Phone GPS is not good enough to set grade stakes. Use a survey GPS or a level from a known point.

## Done when

Stakes set and marked, keep-out zones fenced, files with the operator.
