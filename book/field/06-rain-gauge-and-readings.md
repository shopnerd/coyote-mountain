# Field card 06 · Rain gauge and sensor readings

Rainfall and readings over time, the record the water budget and the recovery checks lean on.

**People:** 1  
**Tool:** coyotemountainfarm.com/topo.html · Site · field notes  

## Set up the gauge

1. Put a straight-sided rain gauge in the open, at least as far from any tree or building as that tree or building is tall.
2. Mount it level on a post, rim about a metre up.
3. Site · field notes: **kind** rain gauge, **measured by** sensor, **note it where I stand**. This is the station.

## Read it

1. Read at the same time every day, early morning is usual.
2. Write the date and the millimetres in a notebook or a phone spreadsheet, two columns: `date, rain mm`. Zero days count: write 0.
3. Empty the gauge.

## Bring readings in

1. Keep the readings as a csv with a date or time column and a value column. A logger's export works the same way.
2. Site · field notes: pick the station in the list, then **import readings · csv**.
3. A chart appears under the note. A soil moisture or pond level logger imported the same way shows rain as bars beneath its own line.

## Good to know

- Missing a day is better than guessing it. Leave it out.
- A snow or hail day: melt it indoors and read the water.
- Moisture probes: same depth, same spots, and note the depth in *what you saw*.

## Done when

A season of readings imported, with the station on the plan.
