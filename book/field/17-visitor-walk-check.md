# Field card 17 · Visitor walk check

Walk a visitor route before the season and after busy weeks: is the ground holding up, and does it need a rest?

**People:** 1  
**Tool:** coyotemountainfarm.com/topo.html · Shape · visitor walks and Site · field notes  

## Walk it

1. Pick the walk in the list. Check its **closed in** months and **when closed, walk** alternative.
2. Walk the whole route at a visitor's pace.
3. At each stop: is the view, sign or feature still there? Is the ground around the stop worn or widening?
4. Along the way, look for:
   - the path widening or doubling where people step around mud
   - new erosion on steep bits (the tool flags slopes steeper than 1 in 5)
   - wet crossings
   - disturbed wildlife: nests, a burrow near the path
5. Note each problem with a photo (card 03).

## Decide

- worn but stable: carry on, check again after the next busy week
- widening or eroding: close it, use the alternative, fix it
- wildlife needs quiet: close it for that season

To close a walk, press the months under **closed in**.

## Log visits

After each group: pick the walk, type **people on this visit**, **log a visit today**. The panel warns when a week goes over the limit.

## Good to know

Limits follow the ground, not a headcount. The weekly limit is a starting point; the wear tells you the real one.

## Done when

The route walked, problems noted, and the walk open or closed on purpose.
