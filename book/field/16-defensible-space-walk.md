# Field card 16 · Defensible space walk

Check the ground around each building against fire, before the dry season.

**People:** 1 or 2  
**Tool:** coyotemountainfarm.com/topo.html · Study · fire ready  

## Before you go

1. **Mark a building** for each house, barn and shed if the map does not show them.
2. **Wind from:** the dangerous wind for the place. **The ground is mostly:** what covers it.
3. Look at the rings on the plan: solid is 30 ft (9 m), dashed is 100 ft (30 m), pushed further out downhill.

## Walk each building

**0 to 5 ft (1.5 m) from the walls**
- nothing that burns: no wood piles, dry leaves, mulch or plants against the walls
- gutters and roof clear

**5 to 30 ft (9 m), the solid ring**
- lean, clean and green: dead plants and dry grass removed, trees pruned up from the ground, space between shrubs

**30 to 100 ft (30 m), the dashed ring**
- reduced fuel: grass kept short, gaps between shrubs and tree crowns, no ladder of plants from ground to tree

**Downhill**
- fire runs uphill: give the downhill side the most clearing, as far as the ring goes

## Also check

- water: a tank or pond an engine can reach, and a hose that reaches every side
- the road in: wide enough, a place to turn around, gates that open

## Record it

A field note per building with photos of each side, and a list of what to clear.

## Good to know

These zones follow the defensible-space guidance used in California. The tool's risk map is a planning aid from slope, sun, wind and cover, not a fire forecast. Ask the local fire service.

## Done when

Every building walked, the clearing list written, and the work scheduled before the dry season.
