# Field card 09 · Plant a row of trees

Put a designed orchard row or hedgerow on the ground at the right spacing.

**People:** 2  
**Tool:** coyotemountainfarm.com/topo.html · Shape · agroforestry  

## Before you go

1. Plan the row: pick the **species**, **row spacing**, and **plant a row: tap its two ends**.
2. Slide **years from planting** up to 15 or 20. Red crosses mean trees that will crowd. Fix the spacing now, not in ten years.
3. Check the flowering calendar for months with nothing for the bees; add a species that fills them.
4. Save the project.

## Bring

- a tape at least as long as the row
- stakes, one per tree, and a mallet
- the tree spacing written down (the species line in the panel gives the canopy range)

## Mark it

1. Flag both ends of the row. For a row on contour, stake it like a swale (card 01). For a straight row, use card 02 to find the ends.
2. Run the tape from end to end.
3. Stake each tree at the spacing along the tape.

## Plant

1. At each stake: dig the hole, plant, backfill, water in.
2. Mulch, and protect young trees from grazing animals.

## Record it

A field note at the first tree: **kind** note, *planted 12 olives, 7 m apart* and the nursery and stock size.

## Good to know

- Canopy sizes and water in the library are typical ranges for a dry site. Your soil and water decide the real ones.
- Young trees need more water than the established figures. Water through the first two dry seasons.

## Done when

Every stake has a tree, watered and protected, and the note is saved.
