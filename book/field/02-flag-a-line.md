# Field card 02 · Flag a line with phone GPS

Walk a designed path, a lot line or a keyline on the ground and flag it, where a few metres either way is fine.

**People:** 1  
**Tool:** coyotemountainfarm.com/topo.html · Make · walk the line  

For a line that must be dead level, use card 01 instead.

## Before you go

1. Draw the line: a path in Shape · path, a line in Shape · sketch, or load the lot in Site · lot and survey.
2. Save the project and open it on the phone (top bar · project · open).
3. Charge the phone. The screen stays on while following.

## Bring

- phone with the project open
- flags or stakes, a mallet
- a tape if you want to check spacing

## Steps

1. **Which lines:** paths, drawn lines, lot line, or keyline and swales. **Line to walk:** the one you want.
2. Press **follow my position**. Wait for the reading to settle. It shows *position good to ±…*.
3. Walk toward the line until it reads a few metres or less. The reading says left or right of the line.
4. Walk along the line, keeping the number small. At each bend and every 10 to 20 m, set a flag and press **drop a flag here**.
5. The reading counts down *… to its end*. Flag the end.

## Good to know

- Phone GPS wanders by a few metres, more under trees and beside cliffs. Stand still for a few seconds before flagging.
- If the reading jumps, wait. Do not chase it.
- No signal: **no gps here? tap the plan to stand** lets you check the plan by hand.

## Done when

- flags at every bend and every 10 to 20 m
- **save flags · gpx** exported and the project saved

## After

Compare the flags with the line on the plan. Move any flag that sits well off a straight run.
