# Field card 13 · Compare two surveys

See where soil was lost and where it built up between two drone surveys: after a storm, a season or an earthwork.

**People:** 1  
**Tool:** coyotemountainfarm.com/topo.html · Study · ground change  

## You need

- two terrain models (DTM GeoTIFFs) of the same ground, flown the same way (card 12)
- the site fetched in the tool around them

## Steps

1. **Before survey:** load the earlier one. **After survey:** load the later one.
2. **Line up heights** stays on unless both flights used ground control.
3. **Ignore changes under:** set it at about the survey's height accuracy. Without ground control, start at 15 cm (0.5 ft).
4. Read the result: ember where soil was lost, blue where it built up, with volumes lost, built up and net.

## Read it carefully

- A patch of change along a vegetation line is usually plants, not soil. Check it on the ground.
- Change all along one edge of the survey is usually a height tilt between flights, not erosion.
- Real erosion shows in the channels, below culverts, on bare slopes and in the pond.

## Record it

A field note at each place that matters, with the volume and a photo from the ground.

## Done when

The main changes are checked on the ground and noted.
