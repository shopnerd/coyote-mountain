# Field card 07 · After-rain walk

Walk the land after a real rain, while the water is still telling you where it goes. The most useful hour of observation there is.

**People:** 1 or 2  
**Tool:** coyotemountainfarm.com/topo.html · Site · field notes, Study · water  

## When

During or within a day of a rain big enough to run off. Note the rain from the gauge (card 06).

## Bring

- phone with the project open, boots, a stick to probe depth
- flags

## Walk

1. Start at the top of the land and follow the water down.
2. At each swale, basin and pond: is it holding? Is it full? Did it overtop, and where?
3. At each road and path: is water running along it or cutting across it?
4. Look for: new rills and gullies, sediment fans, standing water in the wrong place, dry spots that should be wet, springs and seeps.
5. For each, add a field note: **kind** erosion or note, a photo, **note it where I stand**, and what you saw in a few words (*swale 2 overtopped at the west end, 30 cm of scour below*).
6. Flag any spot that needs work.

## Compare with the design

Open Study · water with rain on. Where the model and the ground disagree, the ground is right. Note it.

## Done when

Every swale, basin, road crossing and new erosion spot has a note with a photo.

## After

Fix the overtopping points first. A swale that overtops in one spot cuts a gully there.
