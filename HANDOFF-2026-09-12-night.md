## Day of 12 September (continued, second session)

13. **2x detail photo tiles (`28136a8`).** Will's screenshot showed stripes, a lattice, and pale and dark slivers on ridges. Three causes, each proven with a toggle test. (a) At 2x the grid (10 m) is finer than the 30 m terrarium data, so heights come in plateaus and 1-node shading gradients stair-step. The model's shading gradient now spans `eG = round(W/150)` nodes (`gradM`), identical at 1x. (b) **The lattice was the photo-opacity paper sheet** (`S.aop` defaults to .55): one translucent paper polygon per tile, outset .3 px, so every overlap doubled. (c) Parallelogram photo patches spill past crests. Fix: `shadeTex(src, lx, ly, lz)` bakes once per terrain, light and opacity key. It lays the paper tone (from the light) under the photo at `S.aop`, then multiplies a smooth per-pixel shade canvas over both (4 px per cell, bilinear node gradients). Tiles draw it as a **pattern fill** (`bakedPat.setTransform(DOMMatrix)`): one quad fill when flat, two exact triangles when the tile folds (`warp` > 1.2 px) or is big. Baked tiles get no shade polygons, no paper polygons and no under-fill. Baking runs only outside the render (`!R`; the render tints paper by its own shadow), and not with cut·fill or the overlay raster; those keep the old path. Frames are about a third faster (headless 1.1 s vs 1.6 s at 2x). Render seam score at 1x is 3.0 (was 5.4). **Canvas lesson:** `drawImage` of a sub-rect clamps sampling at the rect, so neighbouring tiles never match at their edges; a pattern fill samples the whole image. `__dbg` gained `z` and `drawModel`. The photo is still soft at 2x: `buildAerial` drops to 6 px/cell when the tile count passes 110.
14. **Animate history, further back (`df778e5`).** `histOlder(keep)` adds three sources before the Wayback photos:
    - **USGS historical topo maps:** the Esri ImageServer `historical1.arcgis.com/.../USA_Historical_Topographic_Maps/ImageServer`. `query` with the site envelope and `esriSpatialRelWithin`, one sheet a year at the finest scale, at most 8, each drawn by `rasterExport()` from one `exportImage` with `esriMosaicLockRaster`.
    - **NASA GIBS Landsat WELD** yearly mosaics for 1984/85/88/89/90/98/99/2000. Tiles at `…/Landsat_WELD_CorrectedReflectance_TrueColor_Global_Annual/default/{yr}-12-01/GoogleMapsCompatible_Level12/{z}/{y}/{x}.jpg`, max zoom 12.
    - **Microsoft Planetary Computer** Landsat 5 scenes for 2003/2007/2011: a STAC POST search (cloud < 5), then item tiles (x before y) with `color_formula=gamma RGB 2.2, sigmoidal RGB 8 0.4`, max zoom 13.

    The plumbing: `rasterFrom(urlOf, ppc, maxZ)` samples bilinearly when the zoom is capped, and `rasterGrid()` is the shared mapping. Frames carry `kind` (map/landsat/photo) and `src`, which shows under the date. `keep()` drops blank sheets (spread < 5 or < 6% ink). `histMatch()` pulls Landsat colour to the photos' average (gain clamped .5–1.6). The ember change pulse runs only photo to photo. Landsat frames are 2 px/cell.

    Results: Encino Solo gets 25 pictures, 1950 → 2025, in about 20 s. The 1950 and 1954 San Diego 1:250,000 sheets reach the site; the 1958 one is blank there and gets dropped. LA (34.05, −118.25) gets 33 pictures from 1894 in about 35 s.
