Ground, drawn from real terrain and painted from the model

Every piece here began as elevation data for a real place, fetched, shaped and viewed in Coyote Mountain Topo, then exported as a drawing sheet, recorded as video, or handed to an image painter as a structured control. Most of it is in the hills near Ensenada, Baja California: Encino Solo, a ranch, and Centro Equino, a horse centre designed in the tool on the real ground.

One view, many ways

Centro Equino · the model first, then paintings of it

Each row is one camera on the model. The first picture is the model itself, with no AI: the ground, the buildings, the roads and the planting as designed. The rest are painters (OpenAI and Google's Gemini) given that picture as a strict control and asked for a time of day or a kind of weather. The model is the truth; the paintings are what it could feel like.
