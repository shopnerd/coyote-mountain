Ground, drawn from real terrain and painted from the model

Every piece here began as elevation data for a real place, fetched, shaped and viewed in Coyote Mountain Topo, then exported as a drawing sheet, recorded as video, or handed to an image painter as a structured control. Most of it is Encino Solo, a ranch near Ensenada in Baja California. Near-duplicates are left out.
