## 2. What changed today (look for these)
**Stable, now 84 × 42 ft.** One more 12 ft bay at the road (west) end: a **kitchen** (NW corner, window to the drive and one
in the north wall), an accessible **bathroom** with shower and the **electrical room** (SW corner, its own outside door).
All 10 stalls and runs unchanged. The wash pad is 12 × 36 under the grape trellis, its trough back at the building's corner.
The **clerestory** now runs almost the whole ridge (76 ft). **Windbreak:** translucent polycarbonate behind the west doors
and gable (the afternoon wind is from the west more than half the time, from 10 years of weather data).
Posts are Andrés' heavy 12 in tubes. → pack pages 3, 4; model.

**Café (the old trailer by the arena), double-sided.** A deck, serving side and three stepped picnic platforms on
**both** long sides, all with **fieldstone risers** and plank tops; one gable roof over the whole thing, 2 ft bigger all
round, **covered in solar panels**; a wide stair at the arena end. At the road end: a deck across the trailer end closed by
a **stone wall** with a wood cap, a **stick screen** (like the stable) up to the peak hiding the trailer end, and a
**horse trough at horse height** in front, fed by the café roof, with tall water plants in its back half. → model; gallery
"cafe-doble" and the café-end camera fan.

**Main road** redrawn from Will's Line Pad trace: the curvy line from the NE gate to the south gate. → model, site sheets.

**Solar on every roof:** stable (both halves), covered stalls (both butterfly planes), café. Full potential 288 panels,
125 kWp, ~197 MWh a year, about 18 times what the centre would use; phase 1 is 16 panels on the stable. → page 19.

**New sheets:**
- **19 · Electrical plan (E-1):** power from the ranch in the water-main trench, main disconnect at the NE gate,
  dark-sky lighting for everything (Ensenada's light rule for the observatory), outlets, solar, the dusk rendering.
- **18 · Irrigation (D-3), updated:** first-flush pipes at every downspout; troughs as natural pools (vetiver in gravel
  over sand, bubbler, goldfish).
- **20 · Reading the site:** wind roses, sun angles, water, fire sector, views; the frameworks (Mollison and Holmgren,
  Geoff Lawton, Steiner, Yeomans).
- **21 · Regenerative design:** zones 0–5, the **compost yard** (where the first outdoor paddocks were drawn), the
  **market garden** for restaurants along the vineyard road, the alfalfa field, cattle and horses as one herd, the vivero,
  biodynamics, natural horsemanship, community and children, and the closed-loops diagram.
- **13 · Topography:** a short note on the site's history as a plant nursery.

**Page layout:** every page now has the same margins and footer; the stable plan and structure pages were re-laid by hand.

## 4. Decisions waiting on you
1. **Pack photos:** which new paintings replace which approved ones (see §3).
2. **East doors:** the coldest winter wind and the Santa Anas come from the **north-east**. Back the east doors too (fixed or removable panels), or leave them open?
3. **Solar:** tie into the ranch's existing grid-tied system, or the centre's own inverter + battery for outages? (page 19 note 5)
4. **Café:** the old trailer is the café on both sides. The Airstream (already a café) could pair with it, or become a guest stay / WWOOF base in the pine grove near the stable. Which?
5. **Alfalfa field and the pond:** the big fenced field south-west of the site (page 21, now labelled), with the pond across the road where all the animals drink and the wild hilly land around it the horses like. Next big piece: **a trail map for all of Chichihuas** (horses, hiking, bikes), with short loops around the pond. What area should it cover, and are there trails or tracks you already use?
6. **Grazing:** page 21 proposes the centre's horses joining the ranch cattle as one herd on planned grazing (Holistic Management). The ranch's call.
