# Handoff: covered stalls, new alfalfa layout

**Project:** Centro Equino, Chichihuas (Valle de Guadalupe, Baja California) · design by Giant Nature
**From:** Walker · **Date:** 4 Oct 2026
**Drawing:** v14 pack, page 10, "Caballerizas techadas · Covered stalls" (`output/Centro-Equino-pack-11x17-2026-10-02-v14.pdf`; page alone in `output/markup/covered-stalls-page10.pdf`)

## What changed
The 12 ft corridor between the covered stalls now runs **straight through the alfalfa end and out the far side**.
Before, a single 16 × 36 ft alfalfa bay closed off that end and had only a gate on its outer side.

## New layout
- **8 stalls of 16 × 20 ft**, 4 per side, on a **12 ft corridor** (unchanged)
- **Alfalfa:** two bays of **16 × 12 ft**, one on each side of the corridor, at the stable end
- **Corridor:** open end to end, from the round trough end through the alfalfa bays and out
- The end wall and gate across the alfalfa bay are removed
- **Overall footprint:** still **80 × 52 ft**
- **Butterfly roof:** still 84 × 36 ft, covering the corridor and the alfalfa end
- **Roof valley, chute and 8 ft round trough:** unchanged

## Things to know
- **Alfalfa storage drops by a third:** from 576 sq ft (16 × 36) to 384 sq ft (two bays of 16 × 12).
- **Horses and the alfalfa:** with the end open, horses can walk past the alfalfa. The bays need a gate or rail on the aisle side to keep horses out of the hay.
- **Unloading:** the notes still say alfalfa unloads "through a gate on the main road". A truck could now back straight into the open aisle. To be decided.
- **Cross-section:** the section on page 10 cuts through the stalls, not the alfalfa end, so it is unchanged.
- **Structure:** the roof frame over the alfalfa end needs checking by the structural engineer (DRO) now that the end wall is gone. No posts or footings have been redrawn.

## Open questions
1. How should the alfalfa bays be closed off from the aisle: a gate, a rail, or a solid wall?
2. Where does the alfalfa get unloaded: from the main road through a gate, or into the open aisle end?
3. Is 384 sq ft enough alfalfa storage, or does it need more space somewhere else?
